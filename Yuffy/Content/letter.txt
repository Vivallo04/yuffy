Dear Princess Polar Bear Yuffy,

I’m writing this letter, one that’s very special and unique, to celebrate our first 100 days together. You are an amazing girl, so full of love, and so determined to support me every day, to hug me, and to give me your love.

Thank you for being part of my everyday life, for listening to me, hugging me, kissing me, and wanting my hugs and kisses more and more each day.

I love seeing you study so hard and daydreaming about our future together. I can never wait to see your precious eyes looking at me with so much love and sweetness, and your open arms that are always ready to hug me whenever I need it.

I truly want to work hard every day so I can invite you to my concerts, live in the same city, cook for each other, spoil one another, and build a life together that is full of love and happiness every single day.

I can’t wait to be with you again — to enjoy our time together, feel each other’s hugs, get to know each other even more, go on romantic dates, buy cute outfits for you, and make you feel loved every day. Let’s work together to love and support each other and to make our relationship joyful and beautiful every day. I want to make my Princess Yuffy happy and surrounded by love every single day.

These are our first days as a couple, learning how to love each other, discovering what we like, and creating our first memories together. We’ve spent our first Christmas, our first New Year, and our first 100 days together. I don’t know if you’re the kind of person who celebrates Valentine’s Day, or if it’s even really celebrated in Taiwan. I’ve never truly celebrated it myself, but I believe that with the right person, it’s more than worth celebrating our love and everything we’ve built together. It’s a beautiful opportunity to create even more memories. It doesn’t matter whether it’s something big or small—just knowing that we’re appreciating each other and celebrating our love will make us happy and give us something to remember and laugh about.

Will you be my Valentine?
