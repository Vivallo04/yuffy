Dear Adventurer,

If you are reading this, then you have proven yourself worthy. The mushrooms you gathered were no ordinary fungi - they hold the essence of this land's ancient magic.

Long ago, the forest spirits scattered them across the fields to test those who would seek the truth. Many have wandered these lands, but few have had the patience and courage to collect them all.

You have earned the right to know: beyond the northern snow, past the frozen pines, lies a hidden grove where the spirits still dance under moonlight. Perhaps one day, you will find your way there.

Until then, keep this letter close. It will guide you when the path grows dark.

- The Forest Keeper
